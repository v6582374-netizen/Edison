# Preserve the complete translation before composing a reading manuscript

The owner wants better translation quality without losing the source's argument in a final editorial pass. Retain the complete faithful translation separately from the reading manuscript, and limit manuscript composition to removing spoken filler and adding hierarchical headings without summarizing away content or reordering the argument. This introduces an additional durable artifact and associated processing cost, accepted to keep fidelity inspectable and prevent later formatting from silently replacing the translation.

[ADR 0019](./0019-preserve-upstream-translation-and-separate-reading-structure.md) strengthens independent translation preservation, fixes upstream ownership and records the owner's updated permissions for light editorial work on a separate reading manuscript.
